# Market Alternatives Research Notes — 25 August 2026

## Source: Sage Nigeria inventory management

URL: https://www.sage.com/en-ng/inventory-management/

Sage describes inventory software as a way to reduce stock-outs and excess stock, automate purchase/replenishment work, maintain live stock data, and support accounting, inventory, operations, distribution, multi-company/multi-currency work, and monthly bank reconciliation. Its product range illustrates the broad, integrated ERP/accounting category against which Control Ledger should not compete feature-for-feature at Release 1.

## Source: Bumpa Nigerian small-business inventory guide

URL: https://www.getbumpa.com/blog/the-5-best-inventory-management-software-for-small-businesses

Bumpa’s guide positions a simple Nigerian merchant tool around product/stock tracking, sales from several channels, staff access, smartphone use, order records, receipts/invoices, and basic analytics. It lists Bumpa, Zoho Inventory, Sortly, Cin7, and Fishbowl as representative inventory options and emphasises local-currency affordability, ease of use, mobile access, integrations, and support. The source is suitable for describing the “simple selling and stock” category, not for making comparative performance claims.

## Source: PepUpSales distributor-management guide

URL: https://www.pepupsales.com/blog/distributor-management-software-for-african-markets/

PepUpSales describes the African distributor-management category as focused on primary/secondary sales visibility, distributor stock, traditional-trade outlets, offline use, field ordering, schemes/promotions, distributor self-service, ERP integration, batch/expiry tracking, and analytics. It identifies unreliable connectivity and the difficulty of tracking warehouse-to-retailer movement as important regional operating realities. Treat these as vendor category descriptions rather than independently verified market statistics.
